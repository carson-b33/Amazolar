Our Programs

Four connected programs that take students from exploring a path to walking it.

Flagship Program

Iluminando Caminos Workshops

Iluminando Caminos (Lighting Paths) is Amazolar's flagship program: a monthly professional development workshop series for students in grades 10–12 and recent graduates. Running through partnerships with local ministry communities in Quito, it mixes real professionals, hands-on activities, and peer reflection to make career readiness feel personal and achievable.
