For Companies

Corporate Sponsorships

Align your company with concrete, visible impact in Quito. Four ways to plug in:

Workshop Sponsorships

Fund one or more monthly workshops directly. A single workshop costs approximately $1,500 and serves 20 students.

Program Cycle Sponsorships

Underwrite an entire 4-month cycle of Iluminando Caminos for a cohort of students — with recognition across the full cycle.

Employer Pipeline Partners

Host company visits, offer summer internships, or build an employer-sponsored talent pipeline with our workshop graduates in Quito.

Technical School & Education Fund

Contribute to the fund that covers technical school scholarships, pre-university tutoring, and internship stipends for students ready to take the next step.

Give Your Time

Volunteering & Internships

We welcome interns and volunteers aged 18 and up — individuals, couples, and families alike. Whether you have a week in Ecuador or a few hours a month from home, there's meaningful work waiting.

Fundraising in the US

Run your own fundraiser — we'll support you with materials and impact stories:

  • Birthday or milestone fundraisers on social media
  • Campus club drives and bake sales
  • Fun runs, game nights, or trivia fundraisers
  • Workplace giving and matching-gift campaigns
  • Dinner parties where the "ticket" is a workshop donation

Opportunities in Ecuador

Short-term: join a monthly workshop as a visiting speaker, activity leader, or extra set of hands — great for travelers and career professionals passing through Quito.

Long-term: commit to a program cycle or more — mentoring students, supporting facilitators, and helping build employer and academy partnerships on the ground.

Opportunities in the US

Open roles we're actively looking to fill remotely:

  • Grant research & writing volunteer
  • Social media & content volunteer
  • Corporate outreach volunteer
  • Translation support (Spanish ↔ English)

For Institutions

Partner with Us

Amazolar works through institutional partners — led by Education = Hope (E=H), our fiscal sponsor and on-the-ground partner in Quito. If your organization, church, school, or business wants to stand behind students in Ecuador, there are three models that work:

Help Us Fundraise

Bring Amazolar to your community — host an event, share our story with your network, or run a giving campaign under your organization's banner.

Match Our Biggest Donations

Multiply momentum by pledging to match major gifts during our fundraising pushes — matching challenges consistently double what a campaign raises.

Percentage of Profit

Commit a percentage of your business's profits or a product line's proceeds to Amazolar — ongoing impact your customers can see and share in.

Talk Partnerships With Us

The most direct way to light a path.
