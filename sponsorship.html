The Mechanics

How Sponsorship Works

1. You choose a tier. Your monthly gift is processed securely through our fiscal sponsor, Education Equals Hope, and is directed to Amazolar's programs in Quito.

2. You receive a photo card. Shortly after you sign up, we send a welcome card with photos from the program your sponsorship supports.

3. You get regular updates. Sponsors receive periodic updates from the field — what workshops covered, who spoke, and how students are progressing along their pathways.

A Closer Connection

Sponsor a Student or a Cohort

Prefer to follow one journey from start to finish? Individual and cohort sponsorships pair you with the students your giving supports.

Individual Sponsorship

Walk alongside one student through a full Iluminando Caminos cycle — from their first workshop to their post-graduation plan. Sponsors receive updates on their student's milestones.

Cohort Sponsorship

Adopt an entire workshop cohort of roughly 20 students. Cohort sponsors receive group updates, photos from each monthly workshop, and an end-of-cycle impact summary.

Get Matched

Email belairec@amazolar.org and we'll match you with a student or cohort beginning their next program cycle.
