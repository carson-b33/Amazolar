The Problem

A diploma without direction isn't an opportunity — it's just a more educated version of the same trap.

In Quito's most underserved communities, students live on family budgets of $2 a day. Many are the first in their families to graduate high school — carrying the hope of finally breaking a cycle of generational poverty. But with no system to prepare them for what comes next, that hope is too often crushed by unemployment, exploitation, substance abuse, or worse.

Our Solution

Iluminando Caminos — one workshop a month, the difference of a lifetime.
