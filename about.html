About Amazolar

Guidance, resources, and community for students building a life of their choosing.

Who We Are

Opportunity shouldn't depend on where you're born.

Amazolar is a mission-driven organization expanding access to higher education and career opportunities for under-resourced students in Latin America. We believe every student deserves the guidance, resources, and community to build a life of their choosing — and that when young people are equipped to imagine and pursue their futures, entire communities transform.

Why We Exist

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To expand access to post-secondary opportunities for under-resourced students by building the agency, knowledge, and skills they need to chart their own path while providing the launchpad for opportunity.

Our Vision

A Latin America where every student — regardless of economic background — graduates with a clear sense of their options, confidence in their abilities, and the support to act on both.
